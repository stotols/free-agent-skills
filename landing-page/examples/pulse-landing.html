Does Pulse read my calendar events?

No. It uses free/busy availability only. Event titles, guests and notes never leave your calendar provider.

What happens when a meeting lands on a session?

The session moves to the next real gap that day, or rolls to tomorrow. It is rescheduled, never silently dropped.

I have tried focus apps and quit them all. Why is this different?

Most focus apps ask you to protect time yourself. Pulse plans inside the calendar you already obey, so the habit it needs from you is one tap at 8:00.
